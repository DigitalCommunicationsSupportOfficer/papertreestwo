A SHOP WITH A LITTLE BIT OF EVERYTHING

Papertrees is more than a single-category shop. Under one roof you will find books, paper, creativity, useful things and the occasional unexpected discovery — gathered together the way a good village shop should be.

Some days you will come in for a printout and leave with a notebook. Other days it might be a birthday card, a paintbrush, or the exact book you had been hoping to find. That is rather the point of us.
